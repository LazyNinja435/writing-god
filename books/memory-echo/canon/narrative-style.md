# Narrative Style

**Status:** CANON

## Voice

Close third-person limited. Precise clinical vocabulary for Sera; slightly rougher diction for Marcus. Avoid exposition dumps; prefer sensory anchors tied to ship environments.

## Tense

Past tense throughout.

## Dialogue

Naturalistic, with subtext. Sera speaks carefully; Marcus deflects with dry humor when threatened.

## POV Rules

No head-hopping within scenes. Sera scenes may not access Marcus's thoughts; Marcus POV scenes (later) likewise restricted.
