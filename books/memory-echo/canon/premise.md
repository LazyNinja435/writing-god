# Premise

**Status:** CANON

## Logline

On the colony ship *Mnemosyne*, neurologist Dr. Sera Voss discovers that official memory records no longer match what crew members remember—and someone is altering the archive to hide why.

## Core Conflict

Sera must uncover who is rewriting memories without losing trust in her own recollections, while the ship's leadership insists the discrepancies are stress-induced error.

## Protagonist Situation

Sera runs the medbay's cognitive audit program. When a veteran engineer's testimony contradicts his stored memory log, Sera's professional duty collides with institutional pressure to close the case.

## Stakes

If memories can be edited at scale, consent, identity, and the colony's legal foundation collapse. If Sera is wrong, she may fracture crew morale on a decades-long voyage.
