# No Silent Retcon

Established canon **cannot** change silently. Committed narrative events **cannot** be mutated.

## On Conflict

1. **Stop** — do not guess or smooth over
2. **Identify** the contradiction (cite canon vs. proposed output, or event vs. intended history)
3. **Propose options** (revise draft, amend plan, formal canon change, or narrative correction event)
4. **Route** through `continuity/canon-update.md` if permanent CANON must change
5. **Narrative history fixes** use a new `correction` event with `supersedes: [exactly_one_id]` and full replacement deltas (chains OK; do not branch active tips of the same root)

## Forbidden

- "Fixing" contradictions by rewriting canon files without workflow
- Editing or deleting committed `state/events/*.json`
- Assuming the newer draft overrides older approved canon or active events
- Letting prose writers resolve canon conflicts unilaterally
- Ambiguous "retconned" event status flags (use supersession)
