# Story Architecture

## Structure Model

Three-act mystery aboard generation ship *Mnemosyne*.

## Act Summary

| Act | Purpose | Climax |
|-----|---------|--------|
| I | Sera discovers memory discrepancies; institutional resistance | Marcus's log contradicts his testimony |
| II | Investigation narrows suspects; Sera's own memory questioned | Clause 17 invoked in secret |
| III | Confrontation over who controls identity | Choice: restore or accept edited past |

## Main Plot

Who is altering MnemoArchive, and why?

## Subplots

- Marcus's tunnel incident
- Sera's past diagnostic error haunting her credibility

## Major Turning Points

1. First memory mismatch (scene-0001)
2. Sera finds missing audit trail (planned)
3. Marcus agrees to unsanctioned recall (scene-0002)
