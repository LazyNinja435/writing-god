# Write Dialogue

## Purpose

Craft or refine dialogue for a scene.

## When to Use

- Dialogue-heavy scenes
- Dialogue review findings

## Required Inputs

- Character voices
- Knowledge boundaries
- Scene goals

## Workflow

1. Match character speech patterns
2. Subtext and conflict in lines
3. Flag knowledge leakage

## Output

Dialogue passages or revised lines

## Forbidden

- Exposition dumps violating character knowledge
