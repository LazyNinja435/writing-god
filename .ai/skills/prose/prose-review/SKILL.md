# Prose Review

## Purpose

Evaluate prose quality without rewriting.

## When to Use

- Scene or chapter review

## Required Inputs

- Manuscript section
- narrative-style.md
- book.yaml prose settings

## Workflow

1. Check clarity, rhythm, voice consistency
2. Note line-level issues
3. Do not rewrite unless protocol allows

## Output

Prose review findings

## Forbidden

- Developmental restructuring during prose review
