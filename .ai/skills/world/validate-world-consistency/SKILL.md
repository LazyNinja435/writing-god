# Validate World Consistency

## Purpose

Check scenes against world rules.

## When to Use

- Continuity review
- Speculative fiction books

## Required Inputs

- World rules canon
- Scene events

## Workflow

1. Match events to rules
2. Flag violations
3. Suggest fixes or canon change

## Output

Review findings

## Forbidden

- Inventing new rules to excuse violations
