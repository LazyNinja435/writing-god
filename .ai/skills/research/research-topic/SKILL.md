# Research Topic

## Purpose

Gather factual research for the book.

## When to Use

- Historical/scientific accuracy needed

## Required Inputs

- Research question
- book.yaml research rules

## Workflow

1. Search reliable sources
2. Distinguish fact from fiction
3. Summarize findings

## Output

research/ notes

## Forbidden

- Treating research as canon
- Uncited claims as fact
