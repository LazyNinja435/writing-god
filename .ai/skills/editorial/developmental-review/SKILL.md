# Developmental Review

## Purpose

Evaluate structure, pacing, and arc progression.

## When to Use

- Scene/chapter/act review

## Required Inputs

- Manuscript section
- Architecture
- Threads

## Workflow

1. Assess scene necessity and stakes
2. Check arc movement
3. Note structural issues

## Output

Developmental review findings

## Forbidden

- Line editing
- Rewriting scenes
