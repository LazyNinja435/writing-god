# Outline Chapter

## Purpose

Plan chapter beats without prose.

## When to Use

- Chapter-level planning

## Required Inputs

- Act outline
- Chapter number
- Thread states

## Workflow

1. List scenes or beat sequence
2. Assign POV if known
3. Note chapter purpose and end hook

## Output

planning/chapters/chapter-<n>.md

## Forbidden

- Writing final prose in outline step
